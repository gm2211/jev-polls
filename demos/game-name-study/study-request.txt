Two-phase study: which of the attached names will make the most money for my game? The game is good; the name has to make people want to find out more.
Phase 1, market research: ask my "Adult gamers able to buy a $20 game" cohort (all 1,000) which name most piques their interest and makes them want to find out more. Use the attached names and descriptions as the options.
Phase 2, board decision: a new cohort of 8 board members, seasoned decision makers with diverse views: business, marketing, advertising, product and finance veterans of launches like StarCraft, Doom, Halo and Crysis, plus a former big-tech CEO. Each reads every individual gamer vote (with the gamer's segment) and picks the name we should ship.
Final: a new cohort of 1 synthesis chair who reads all 8 board votes and gives one unified, weighted ranking of the names.
